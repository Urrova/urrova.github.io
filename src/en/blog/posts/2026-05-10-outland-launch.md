**Johnny Cruelty/Grungo has released the first episode of OUTLAND**, a planned 32-maps TC for Doom II. It's intended to be 6 episodes of 5 maps + 1 secret maps. It's compatibility is **MBF21 (and uses DECOHACK)**, so it's playable on the [DSDA Doom](https://github.com/kraflab/dsda-doom) Sourceport.

I've played it, and i can say this wad is **ABSOLUTE CINEMA**{ .text_yellow }. The maps are loooooooong, from the start you're welcomed with a HUGE open map on a city. Just look at this:
